  Apparent horizon r_AH(v) = 2M(v): local, reads the mass function
  alone.  Event horizon r_EH(v): global, found by shooting outgoing
  light rays to the far future.  r_EH >= r_AH always; the two
  coincide only once the mass has stopped growing (v > v2), and the
  event horizon starts rising measurably *before* the accretion
  begins (v < v1) -- it anticipates mass that has not arrived yet.
----------------------------------------------------------------------

        the bisection bracket has been narrowed to a width of about
        1.23e-16 r_s0 (60 iterations); this measures only the
        bisection's own precision, not how close the shooting method's

        of error.
