Purpose
-------
These four figures give a quick visual survey of the program's four modes.
They are intended for evaluation by Prof. Bernard Schutz and Vince Higgs,
Physics and Astronomy, Cambridge University Press. The program is a transparent
teaching model combining stated differential equations, algebraic derivations,
empirical fits, and calibrated closures. It is not a full stellar-evolution
code, and the plots label the important limitations directly.

1. SINGLE SOLAR-MASS EVOLUTIONARY TRACK
   Figure: figures/sev_track_1.00Msun_20260822_165141.png

   Command:
   python main.py --mode tracks --mass 1.0 --outdir ./figures --dpi 150

   What it shows:
   - Motion across the Hertzsprung-Russell diagram from the ZAMS to helium
     ignition.
   - Luminosity and radius histories.
   - Central-hydrogen depletion and helium-core growth.
   - Explicit separation between the integrated track and the schematic
     eventual-remnant classification.

   Headline values:
   - Main-sequence lifetime: 9.733 Gyr.
   - Helium ignition: total age 11.72 Gyr, core mass 0.470 solar masses.
   - Giant-branch-tip luminosity: approximately 2479 solar luminosities.

2. HR DIAGRAM WITH TRACKS AND ISOCHRONES
   Figure: figures/sev_hr_20260822_165141.png

   Command:
   python main.py --mode hr --masses 0.8,1.0,1.5,2.0,3.0,5.0,10.0 \
       --isochrones 0.1,1,5,10 --t_max 60 --outdir ./figures --dpi 150

   What it shows:
   - Seven mass-dependent evolutionary tracks on a conventional HR diagram.
   - Schematic teaching isochrones at 0.1, 1, 5, and 10 Gyr.
   - The strong dependence of lifetime and turn-off mass on stellar mass.
   - Empirical ZAMS anchoring and the distinct low- and high-mass post-main-
     sequence prescriptions.

   Turn-off masses:
   - 0.1 Gyr: 6.286 solar masses.
   - 1 Gyr:   2.290 solar masses.
   - 5 Gyr:   1.260 solar masses.
   - 10 Gyr:  0.991 solar masses.

3. WHITE-DWARF STRUCTURE AND MESTEL COOLING
   Figure: figures/sev_wdcool_0.60Msun_20260822_165244.png

   Command:
   python main.py --mode wdcool --wd_mass 0.6 \
       --outdir ./figures --dpi 150

   What it shows:
   - The cold Chandrasekhar mass-radius relation from the exact ideal,
     zero-temperature electron equation of state in Newtonian hydrostatic
     equilibrium.
   - A 0.6-solar-mass model compared with the Earth radius and Chandrasekhar
     limit.
   - Numerical RK4 Mestel cooling and the asymptotic L proportional to
     t^(-7/5) law.
   - Core and surface temperatures and the white-dwarf cooling path on the HR
     diagram.

   Headline values:
   - Radius: 8839.5 km, or 1.387 Earth radii.
   - Central density: 3.21 x 10^9 kg/m^3.
   - Cooling from core temperature 3 x 10^7 K to 3 x 10^6 K: 5.624 Gyr in
     this classical model.

4. NEUTRON-STAR MASS-RADIUS SEQUENCE
   Figure: figures/sev_nsmr_polytrope_20260822_165142.png

   Command:
   python main.py --mode nsmr --eos polytrope \
       --outdir ./figures --dpi 150

   What it shows:
   - TOV integrations for a toy stiff polytropic equation of state.
   - The mass-radius curve and the turning point that separates stable and
     unstable branches for this cold, non-rotating, one-parameter sequence.
   - Black-hole and Buchdahl reference curves.
   - Compactness, surface gravitational redshift, and the approximately
     2-solar-mass observational benchmark.

   Headline values:
   - Maximum mass: 2.175 solar masses.
   - Radius at maximum mass: 11.69 km.
   - Surface redshift at maximum mass: 0.490.
   - Peak sound speed on the stable branch: 0.836 c.

Notes for evaluation
--------------------
- All four runs also print detailed terminal summaries.
- Adding --csvdir PATH writes timestamped, self-documenting CSV tables without
  suppressing the screen display.
- Adding --outdir PATH saves each timestamped figure and still displays it.
- --no_plot --csvdir PATH provides a data-only mode for sweeps.
- The full help file explains which results are integrated, derived, fitted,
  or prescribed and supplies exercises from introductory through advanced.
