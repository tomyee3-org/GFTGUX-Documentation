Beat 0 — A 1.4+1.4 solar-mass inspiral from 20 Hz

Exact command:

python main.py --outdir ./figures

What it shows

  • The top trace and its ±A(t) envelope cover the full 158-second calculated band.
  • The middle panel isolates the inspiral amplitude scale; it does not extend beyond cutoff.
  • The bottom panel shows f(t) steepening toward the chosen Schwarzschild ISCO.

Headline values

  • Masses: 1.400+1.400 M☉; chirp mass: 1.2188 M☉.
  • Distance: 400.0 Mpc; start: 20.0 Hz.
  • Time to ISCO: 157.868 s; cutoff: 1570.4 Hz.
  • Face-on A at cutoff: 5.583e-23; 789,341 inspiral samples.

Beat 1 — A heavier 36+29 solar-mass inspiral

Exact command:

python main.py --m1 36 --m2 29 --d 440 --outdir ./figures

What it shows

  • The frequency rises and the waveform becomes more closely spaced in time.
  • The leading-order strain scale grows as the signal approaches its cutoff.
  • Both chirp mass and total mass differ from Beat 0; distance changes too.

Headline values

  • Masses: 36.000+29.000 M☉; chirp mass: 28.0956 M☉.
  • Distance: 440.0 Mpc; start: 20.0 Hz.
  • Time to ISCO: 0.813 s; cutoff: 67.6 Hz.
  • Face-on A at cutoff: 1.165e-21; 4,065 inspiral samples.

Beat 2 — A zoomed illustrative Schwarzschild ringdown

Exact command:

python main.py --m1 36 --m2 29 --d 440 --ringdown --t_before 0.2 --t_after 0.05 --outdir ./figures

What it shows

  • Blue inspiral and red toy ringdown meet at the dotted cutoff line.
  • The middle and bottom panels describe inspiral quantities only.
  • The plot itself labels the QNM extension as illustrative, not a physical merger.

Headline values

  • Inspiral ends after 0.813 s at 67.6 Hz.
  • Assumed nonspinning final mass: 61.750 M☉.
  • Illustrative QNM frequency: 195.5 Hz.
  • QNM amplitude-decay time: 3.417 ms.

Notes for evaluation

  • Execute the exact command above in the folder containing main.py to reproduce the corresponding printout and figure.
  • The default full-band plot packs many cycles into one screen; use Matplotlib's zoom or --t_before for the last cycles. The lower-right toolbar gives cursor coordinates in the active panel.
  • Only the blue waveform is calculated as leading-order inspiral; the red QNM is prescribed and never represents a computed merger.
  • PNG and CSV output may be saved with --outdir and --csvdir; the latter contains numerical samples and metadata in the columns t_s,f_hz,A,h,phase_rad.
