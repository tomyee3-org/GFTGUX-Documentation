Cosmology Expansion Simulator

Current program version: 1.5.0   Build: 9ef1e60257d3

Purpose. These four figures give a compact visual survey of the program’s three modes. The simulator evaluates the FLRW background equations for a homogeneous, isotropic universe containing matter, radiation, curvature, and dark energy, then solves the expansion history numerically. The governing equations are exact within that model; integration, root-finding, and quadrature carry ordinary numerical error. The CPL equation of state, w(a) = w₀ + wₐ(1−a), is a phenomenological model for unknown dark-energy physics.

1. Flat ΛCDM: a Planck-2018-like concordance cosmology

  • The Hubble parameter falls by many orders of magnitude and approaches its de Sitter asymptote for this constant-Λ model.
  • The radiation → matter → dark-energy sequence of fractional density parameters.
  • The deceleration parameter crosses zero at the onset of accelerated expansion.

2. A closed, recollapsing universe

Figure: cosmo_evolve_20260823_095955_089616.png

python main.py --mode evolve --omega_m 1.5 --omega_de 0.0 --continue_collapse --outdir ./figures

Representative output supplied with the project and embedded directly in this guide.

What it shows

  • A matter-dominated, positively curved universe that expands, turns around, and recollapses.
  • The contracting branch follows from the exact time-reversal symmetry of the Friedmann equation and has negative H.
  • The symmetric-log H-axis makes the expansion-to-contraction sign change visible.
  • The deceleration parameter diverges as turnaround is approached because H tends to zero while the densities remain finite.

Headline values

  • Turnaround: a_turn = 2.9995 at t = 93.06 Gyr.
  • Total lifetime from Big Bang to Big Crunch: 186.1 Gyr.
  • Age when a = 1: 8.538 Gyr.
  • The run stopped at a genuine turnaround.

4. The historical “age crisis”: age versus Ωₘ₀

Figure: cosmo_age_scan_20260823_100029_976039.png

python main.py --mode age --scan_param omega_m --scan_lo 0.05 --scan_hi 1.5 --scan_n 50 --outdir ./figures

Representative output supplied with the project and embedded directly in this guide.

What it shows

  • The age of a flat universe as Ωₘ₀ is varied, compared with a 13 Gyr reference age.
  • The dimensionless product H₀t₀, with Einstein-de Sitter and Milne reference values.
  • The classic result that a high-density matter universe can be younger than its oldest stars.
  • Models above Ωₘ₀ ≈ 1 are marked when they reach a = 1 and recollapse within the scan’s finite a = 5 look-ahead horizon.

Headline values

  • The 13 Gyr line is crossed near Ωₘ₀ ≈ 0.3–0.4.
  • Einstein-de Sitter gives H₀t₀ = 2/3.
  • Milne gives H₀t₀ = 1.
  • Recollapse markers report the finite scan horizon, not a proof about arbitrarily distant future evolution.

Notes for evaluation

  • Every run prints a detailed terminal summary, including the actual stopping condition and warnings when a model is pushed outside typical bounds.
  • --csvdir PATH writes timestamped, self-documenting CSV tables without suppressing the screen display.
  • --outdir PATH saves a timestamped figure and still displays it.
  • --no_plot --csvdir PATH provides a data-only workflow for parameter sweeps.
  • The program distinguishes exact GR equations, algebraic identities, phenomenological assumptions, and numerical results.
  • The age scan’s recollapse flag is explicitly tied to its finite a = 5 look-ahead horizon.
