Beat 4 · Face-on transfer functions

For the near-critical EXP-10 cursor exercise, run python main.py --mode transfer in a graphical Matplotlib session. The saved figure below has no live (x, y) readout; use the toolbar magnifier or enlarge the interactive window. In the left panel the live coordinates are impact parameter and crossing radius (both divided by M); in the right panel they are source radius and emitted intensity.

python main.py --mode transfer --outdir ./figures

What it shows

  • The direct crossing is m = 1.
  • Later crossings approach the critical impact parameter.
  • Some rays do not have a third crossing in the sampled domain.

Headline values

  • r_hot = 6 M
  • Adaptive b samples = 192
  • Finite r_3 samples = 110
  • b(r_m = r_hot)/M = 6.9321, 5.4789, 5.208

Beat 5 · Point-sampled face-on image

In the live image window, hovering over the radial intensity panel reports impact parameter and image intensity; hovering over one of the three face-on panels reports two image-plane coordinates. The saved PNG below does not have a live cursor readout.

python main.py --mode image --outdir ./figures

What it shows

  • The left panel includes direct emission.
  • Later panels include the lensing and higher-order crossings.
  • The narrow combined high-order peak is resolved in the radial profile but can be poorly sampled by image pixel centres. The cyan critical curve is sparsely dotted to leave gaps through which the image can be seen.
  • The inferno palette represents intensity in false color, not observed wavelength.

Headline values

  • g^4 at r_hot = 0.444444; with --r_hot 8: 0.5625
  • b_m(r_m = r_hot)/M = 6.9321, 5.4789, 5.208, 5.1967; at 8 M: 8.9467, 5.588, 5.2119, 5.1968
  • Maximum table I_2 / I_(m=3,4) = 0.4457 / 0.4457; at 8 M: 0.5629 / 0.5629
  • Maximum raster m ≥ 3 increment = 0.07043; at 8 M: 0.0835

Beat 8 · Two meanings of a ring

python main.py --mode compare --outdir ./figures

What it shows

  • The galaxy calculation uses a transparent thin lens.
  • The hole’s boundary follows strong-field capture.
  • Only b_crit is drawn on the image plane; the galaxy's Einstein radius is given as a number in units of its own GM/c².

Headline values

  • Galaxy log10(M/M_sun) = 12; second command: 12.5
  • theta_E = 2.018 arcsec; at 12.5: 3.588 arcsec
  • R_E / (GM/c²)_galaxy = 204432; at 12.5: 114960
  • b_crit/M (this hole) = 5.19615

Notes for evaluation

The image is a static, face-on, optically thin teaching model around a nonrotating black hole. The capture map assumes a distant spherical screen. The intensity palette is false color. Higher image orders beyond m = 4 are omitted from the displayed image; its pixels are point samples. Use the radial profile to inspect narrow peaks.
