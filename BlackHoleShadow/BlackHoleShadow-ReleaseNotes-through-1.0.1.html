Release Status

Version 1.0.0 is the first numbered release offered for consideration by Prof. Bernard Schutz and Vince Higgs as a possible addition to the second-edition program suite of Gravity from the Ground Up. It follows a kickoff implementation modeled on PhotonOrbit and GravitationalLensing, then twenty rounds of independent review by Claude, Codex, and Copilot, with Grok applying the accepted repairs. The reviewers checked the Schwarzschild capture map, the critical curve, face-on transfer functions, the optically thin image, near-critical quadrature, Help synchronization, BUILD_ID provenance, and scale invariance. Priority-1 numerical defects found during that cycle have been closed. Remaining Priority-2 items are software-polish and maintainability issues, not defects in the silhouette physics or in the student-facing teaching beats. The program author has judged the build good enough to present, and has approved release of 1.0.0 with those items tracked below as Known Minor Maintenance Items.

What the program is

BlackHoleShadow is an image-plane teaching program for a nonrotating hole. Nine --mode drawings share one entry point. Length flags are in units of M. The default spacetime scale is M = 1. The geometric shadow is the disk b ≤ 3√3 M on a distant spherical screen. A static, face-on, optically thin Gaussian annulus then shows why that disk is not a thin-lens Einstein ring: the same source appears at several image-plane radii, and the higher-order images hug the critical curve.

Major Improvements in This Release

  • Nine CLI modes (rays, pixels, capture, ring, transfer, image, radii, weak, compare) follow the Help-file teaching beats from two demonstration geodesics through the capture map, photon-ring overlay, transfer functions, and a point-sampled face-on image.
  • Impact-parameter classification uses the exact Schwarzschild critical value b_crit = 3√3 M. Equality is the unstable circular photon orbit and is treated as captured for the silhouette (a set of measure zero on the grid). Public inbound azimuth at that exact value uses the analytic critical geodesic critical_phi_of_x; the endpoint angle is infinite.
  • Face-on equatorial crossings r_m(b) are inverted from the inbound null azimuth. The student image sums static g4 I_em through m = 1..MAX_IMAGE_M (currently 4). Orders m ≥ 5 are omitted from the raster and disclosed on the figure and in the console summary.
  • SciPy adaptive quadrature is the primary engine for ordinary rays. Near the critical impact parameter, periapsis and azimuth live in utilities_bhs.py at MP_DPS (30 digits) and stay in mpmath until a designated float64 exit. Ordinary rays keep a float64 / Dekker fast path for β and ε = β2 − 27.
  • Awkward-mass first-representable rays (math.nextafter(b_crit, ±∞) at M = 0.023, 0.139, 0.278, and 1) keep the correct capture sign, a high-order m = 12 crossing when requested, and M-invariant r/M and b/M.
  • A deterministic BUILD_ID hashes five executable modules (physics_bhs.py, utilities_bhs.py, driver_bhs.py, main.py, plot_bhs.py). --sync-help writes Version and Build into the Help file. Documentation and tests are intentionally excluded from that hash.
  • Length flags on the command line are dimensionless ratios of M. Even --n_pix values become the next odd integer so a pixel centre sits on the optical axis.
  • Help-file language no longer depends on a PhotonOrbit experiment number. Near-critical whirling is described as a set of directions on the sky rather than as one trajectory.

Test Suite Growth

A regression suite was built at kickoff and grew through twenty audit rounds to 93 automated tests in tests/test_physics_bhs.py. The suite locates the nearest directory that contains all five core module filenames, so the same file runs from the canonical tests/ layout or a flattened upload layout. It checks capture classification, periapsis, deflection versus the weak-field 4M/b formula, camera grids, photon-ring masks, transfer and image tables, Help synchronization and MathJax delimiter balance, BUILD_ID coverage including a missing-file failure path, ambient mpmath-dps isolation, and scale invariance at non-unit M.

A representative command is MPLBACKEND=Agg python -W error::ResourceWarning -m unittest tests.test_physics_bhs from the program folder. SciPy, NumPy, Matplotlib, and mpmath are required; see requirements.txt.

Known Limitations

  • Intended for education. It is not an EHT pipeline, a Kerr ray-tracer, or a research-grade image-reconstruction code.
  • This version is face-on and static. There is no orbital Doppler boost, no inclination other than zero, and no optically thick disk.
  • The published image truncates at MAX_IMAGE_M = 4. Higher-order crossings exist and are used as robustness targets; they are omitted from the student raster and labeled as such.
  • Pixels are point samples at cell centres. Narrow m = 3,4 peaks may miss a centre. The radial I(b) panel is the place to read those peaks.
  • A captured backward ray is stopped at the horizon. It may intersect the toy disk first; that is a property of the source model, not a second shadow.
  • The Help file loads MathJax from a public CDN. Offline, the prose and unrendered TeX remain readable, but equations are not typeset.
  • Atomic publication of PNG plus a provenance sidecar is ordinary single-user file I/O, not the hardened CSV/PNG protocol used in GravitationalWaveSources.

Known Minor Maintenance Items

  • Several float64 near-critical helpers (_signed_eps, _beta_offset, _beta_sq_minus_27, _two_square) remain in physics_bhs.py for the ordinary-ray fast path rather than being folded into utilities_bhs.py. That split is deliberate after Audit 20; further DRY is optional cleanup.
  • utilities_bhs.py still carries a long module preamble written during the audit cycle. Shortening it to present-tense student rationale is tracked for a maintenance round.
  • The forced SciPy-fallback unit test exercises the large-absolute-error branch. A separate warning-plus-relative-error trigger remains untested in isolation.
  • The full suite can take on the order of one to two minutes, depending on host, because high-order near-critical inversions are part of the contract.
  • Priority-2 items that survived late audits (plot-label synchronization for unused max_m values, repository-path wording, missing-file BUILD_ID behavior) were addressed in 0.21.0; any leftovers are documentation tone rather than incorrect physics.
