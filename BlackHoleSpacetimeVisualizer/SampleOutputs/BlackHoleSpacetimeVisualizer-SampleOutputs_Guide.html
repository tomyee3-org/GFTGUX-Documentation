Beat 0 — Flamm’s paraboloid: the spatial throat

Exact command:

python main.py --mode embed --M 10 --outdir ./figures

What it shows

  • The upper profile rises from the Schwarzschild throat.
  • The dashed lower profile is a mirrored drawing aid.
  • The surface of revolution depicts spatial geometry, not time or a passage.

Headline values

  • Mass 10 M☉; Schwarzschild radius 29.53 km.
  • Plotted to 8 r_s with 400 radial points.
  • Gaussian curvature at the horizon: −5.7328e−10 m⁻².

Beat 1 — Tidal acceleration at two black-hole masses

Exact command:

python main.py --mode tidal --M 10 --compare_masses 10,4.31e6 --outdir ./figures

What it shows

  • The left panels sample the tide at 1.01–8 r_s for a 10 M☉ hole.
  • The comparison panels use each mass’s own horizon radius.
  • The 100 g line is illustrative and says nothing definitive about survival.

Headline values

  • Test separation: 1.8 m.
  • At 10 M☉: radial horizon tide 1.8914e7 g; threshold radius 57.4 r_s.
  • At 4.31e6 M☉: radial horizon tide 1.0182e−4 g; threshold radius 0.01006 r_s.

Beat 2 — Radial infall: two times and outward redshift

Exact command:

python main.py --mode infall --M 10 --r0_rs 6 --outdir ./figures

What it shows

  • The first panels track radius and Schwarzschild coordinate time against proper time.
  • The bottom panels show local static-frame speed and the frequency ratio at infinity.
  • No sample is on or within the horizon.

Headline values

  • Release at 6 r_s; final radius 1.0005 r_s.
  • Proper time 2.2048475 ms; coordinate time 3.3868254 ms.
  • Final speed 0.999700 c; frequency ratio 2.7377e−4.

Beat 3 — Apparent and event horizons during accretion

Exact command:

python main.py --mode horizons --M0 5 --M1 10 --outdir ./figures

What it shows

  • The left plot contrasts the two horizon radii across the growth interval.
  • The right plot shows nearby outgoing null-ray trajectories.
  • The reported event horizon is the backward-integrated generator, not a forward trial.

Headline values

  • Initial and final masses: 5 and 10 M☉.
  • Accretion window v/r_s0=5–15; plotted interval −20–30.
  • At v_start/r_s0=−20: r_EH/r_s0=1.00000145.
  • Forward shooting reaches v2 here; maximum curve difference 4.156e−5 r_s0.

Notes for evaluation

The program shows each figure and saves a timestamped PNG when --outdir is set. A CSV can be requested with --csvdir ./data; for CSV alone, combine --no_plot with --csvdir. A normal figure viewer’s Matplotlib status bar reports cursor coordinates in the active axes; use those scale readings rather than measuring lengths in page pixels. The 100 g tide is an assumed cutoff, and the infall plot stops outside the horizon.
