Cosmology Expansion Simulator 1.5.0 Release Notes

Version: 1.5.0
Build: 9ef1e60257d3
Date: 2026-08-24

Release Status

Version 1.5.0 has undergone multiple independent review rounds involving Codex, Copilot, Gemini, and Claude responses. Major numerical and physics-correctness issues identified during earlier audits were corrected before this release.

Major Improvements Since 1.3.x

  • Contracting-branch endpoint handling corrected.
  • Big-Bang connectivity certification strengthened.
  • Corrected CPL asymptotic coefficient treatment.
  • Added past-eternal model classification.
  • Added structured fate reporting and crossing tracking.
  • Added BUILD_ID provenance support.
  • Expanded regression suite to 91 tests.

Known Limitations

  • Extreme CPL parameter choices may exceed representability policies.
  • Some extreme-parameter tests may still emit RuntimeWarning messages even when final classification is correct.
  • Crossing detection uses practical sign-change scanning and may not resolve arbitrarily close crossings.
  • The simulator is intended for education and exploration rather than precision cosmological parameter estimation.

Audit Summary

  • Audit 7: Contracting-branch endpoint and provenance improvements verified.
  • Audit 8: Big-Bang connectivity and CPL asymptotic fixes verified.
  • Audit 9: Past-eternal classification, build-ID, and new regression coverage verified; one RuntimeWarning cleanup item remains.
